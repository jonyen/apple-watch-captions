Privacy Policy

Captions collects nothing about you, sends nothing anywhere, and has no servers to send it to.

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The short version

Everything the app does happens on your Apple Watch and your iPhone. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no backend service operated by the developer or anyone else. The developer cannot see your conversations, because they never leave your devices.

What the app handles

DataWhere it goesHow long it is kept
Microphone audio Processed on your Apple Watch, or sent to your paired iPhone over the encrypted Bluetooth link between them for more accurate transcription. It is never written to permanent storage and never transmitted anywhere else. Discarded as it is transcribed; nothing remains when a session ends.
Caption text Shown on your watch, and on your iPhone while a session is running. Discarded when the session ends, unless you chose to keep it.
Saved transcripts and summaries Stored on your devices only, in the apps' private storage. Summaries are generated on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model; no text is sent to any online service. Until you delete them. The watch keeps only recent sessions; your iPhone keeps the full set.
Anything you export Wherever you choose to send it using the iOS share sheet — for example Notes, Files, or Mail. That destination's own privacy policy then applies. Controlled by you and the app you sent it to.
Tips (optional) Handled entirely by Apple's In-App Purchase system. The developer receives no payment details and cannot identify who tipped. See Apple's privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage measurement.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.
  • No audio recordings are stored, on your device or anywhere else.

Other people in your conversations

Captions transcribes speech it hears, which may include people other than you. Their words are handled exactly as described above and are never uploaded. Laws about recording or transcribing other people differ from place to place, and some require everyone's consent. Please use the app accordingly.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the app ever transmitted anything off your devices, that would be stated plainly here and in the app before it happened.
